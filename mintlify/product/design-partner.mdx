title: "Start from a syllabus"
description: "Review a syllabus with Design partner, preview a draft course, and keep the parts you want."

Design partner helps an instructor turn a syllabus into a course draft. The instructor remains the subject-matter expert and instructor of record. The partner handles sequencing, alignment, scaffolding, and quality checks. Every new block remains a draft until a person keeps it; publishing is a separate decision.

## Start and read

Open a course and choose **Start from a syllabus**. Upload a PDF or DOCX, choose a course file, or paste text. In demo mode, use the fictional sample syllabus or paste text. Confirm that the source is a syllabus or training brief and that Design partner may read it. It does not take rosters, grades, or student work as input.

The **instructional read** shows the extracted course profile, outcomes, assessment alignment, weekly workload, and questions about gaps or conflicts. Each claim includes a page or section citation where the source provides one. Correct a field or answer a question before confirming the outcomes. An outcome rewrite is an optional draft; the original wording stays visible.

## Choose and preview

Compare the proposed **approaches**. Choose one or combine approaches, then explain why the selection fits your students. Optional bookends add Start-here and Wrap-up modules. **Preview** lists the modules, lessons, assignments, objectives, workload estimates, overlaps with existing content, and the readiness forecast. It creates nothing until you apply the displayed change set. If an assessment lacks clear points, confirm them before applying.

## Review the draft course

After applying, open the course outline and review each new module and lesson. The lesson editor's **Review with Design partner** panel explains why the module was proposed, cites the syllabus, and gives buttons for missing readings, instructor examples, overlapping lessons, and assessment weights. It can draft a scenario, a worked example, a video script, a short version, or a plain-language version. These remain drafts.

For the module you marked least sure, its first lesson has two alternative openings. Compare them, keep one, and revert the other. The course readiness report and review counts update as you keep blocks. You can download the full design record as JSON or the course map as CSV.

**Undo the plan** removes untouched drafts created by that plan. Content you kept or edited stays, along with its lesson and module. Existing course content is never removed by the plan.

## Institution policy and external agents

An administrator can allow or disable Start from a syllabus, limit which approaches can be suggested, edit six workload assumptions, and set the default AI-use disclosure for the draft Start-here lesson. An instructor can edit that disclosure before keeping its block.

Scoped MCP tools let an external assistant create, read, answer, confirm, select, preview, apply, and undo a design session. The same role, course-access, and token-scope checks apply as in the API. Assistants cannot keep blocks or publish lessons; those decisions stay with people. See [MCP server](/build/mcp).
